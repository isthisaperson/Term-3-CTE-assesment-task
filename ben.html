What You'll Learn

Through this game, players learn how to make smart decisions when dealing with computer safety and cybersecurity. Every file, message and pop-up is based on common dangers found in modern computing, including viruses, phishing scams, suspicious downloads and adware. Players will learn how to identify potential threats and understand the risks of interacting with them.

Along the way, players practise protecting private information, recognising safe and unsafe files, avoiding scams, managing passwords and removing harmful software. Using in-game features such as the password manager, phishing pop-ups and trash bin, players can put their knowledge into practice and develop skills that can be useful when using real computers.

User Guide

How to Build Your PC

Choose Components: Select a CPU, GPU, RAM, storage, motherboard, power supply, and case for your build.

Install Components: Place each component into the correct position in your PC.

Complete Your Build: Make sure all your components are compatible before starting your system.

Choosing Components

CPU: Controls how quickly your computer can process tasks.

GPU: Handles graphics and has a major impact on gaming performance.

RAM: Gives your computer more room to handle multiple tasks.

Storage: Stores your operating system, games, and files.

Compatibility

Not every component works with every PC. Check that your CPU matches your motherboard, your RAM is supported, your power supply can handle your components, and everything fits inside your case.

Performance

Your component choices affect the final performance of your PC. A powerful GPU can improve gaming performance, while a faster CPU and enough RAM can help your system handle demanding tasks.
