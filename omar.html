About the Game

Descend into the depths of a mysterious dungeon as a lone knight destined for greatness. Cyber Dungeon – Animated Edition is an action-packed cybersecurity game where your wits are your weapon. Battle through four levels of increasingly dangerous enemies — orcs, soldiers, and spiders — by correctly identifying real and fake emails to unlock your attacks. Dodge fireballs, survive explosions, and uncover the secrets of the dungeon. The villagers await your return.

Game Objective

Fight through four dungeon levels by identifying phishing emails correctly to defeat enemies and progress to the next stage.

Loop of Gameplay:

• Click the ✓ if an email is real, click the ✗ if it is fake

• Correct answers deal damage to enemies; wrong answers cost you

• Once the portal opens, press Space to advance to the next level

• Dodge incoming fireballs using the Arrow Keys

• Defeat all enemies across four levels to achieve victory

• A win screen is unlocked once all levels are cleared

User Guide

Controls

Mouse Click: Click ✓ (real email) or ✗ (fake email) to interact with each prompt.

Space Bar: Press Space once the portal opens to move to the next level.

Arrow Keys: Dodge incoming fireballs during combat phases.

Levels Overview

• Level 1 — The Orc Lair: Battle orcs by correctly sorting emails. Introduction to basic phishing cues.

• Level 2 — The Soldier's Keep: Face soldier enemies with trickier email scenarios.

• Level 3 — The Spider's Web: Spider enemies add fireball mechanics. Dodge while you answer.

• Level 4 — The Final Depths: The ultimate challenge — all threats combined. Reach the win screen to complete your quest.

Identifying Real vs Fake Emails

A real company will always use correct spelling and will never threaten to permanently delete your account without consent. Look out for spelling errors, urgency tactics, and suspicious sender details — these are signs of a phishing attempt. Click ✗ to reject fake emails and ✓ to approve real ones.

Winning & Losing Conditions

Win: Correctly identify emails, defeat all enemies across all four levels, and reach the Victory screen.

Lose: Taking too many hits from incorrect answers or fireballs will result in defeat.

What You'll Learn

Cyber Dungeon teaches players to tell the difference between genuine and fraudulent emails from companies. By spotting spelling mistakes, suspicious urgency, and unreasonable threats — such as an email claiming to permanently delete your account without consent — players build real-world skills they can apply whenever they receive a suspicious message. Every in-game email is modelled on real phishing tactics, making the lessons directly transferable to everyday life online.
